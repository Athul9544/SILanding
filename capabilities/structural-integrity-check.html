Structural Integrity Check & Improvement

Find structural weak points in a model and strengthen them before they reach site.

SketchUp Intelligence reads the model as a structure, not just a set of surfaces. It follows how loads travel through walls, slabs and supports, points out the members doing more work than they should, and suggests changes that keep the design intent intact.
